## Description

Iron Pillar is the workout tracker that makes showing up feel like progress.

FOLLOW A PROGRAM
Pick a plan built for you: 3-Day Full Body, 4-Day Upper/Lower, Push/Pull/Legs, or a 2-Day Minimal plan for busy weeks. Home always shows your next day, so you never wonder what to do.

SEE PROPER FORM
Every major lift shows start and finish photos and short coaching cues, right where you log your sets. Not sure about a machine? Swap to an alternative that works the same muscles.

LOG FAST
Tap to log a set with your numbers from last time already filled in. Weight carries down from set to set, the rest timer starts on its own, and if you have to leave mid-workout your sets are saved so you can pick up where you left off.

WEEKLY GOALS, NOT DAILY GUILT
Choose 2 to 6 workouts a week. Hit your goal and the week counts toward your streak. Rest days never break it, and one missed week a month is forgiven.

RANK UP
Every set, personal record and session where you beat last time earns XP. Climb seven tiers from Iron to Pillar. Your rank only ever goes up.

TRAIN WITH FRIENDS
Add friends, see their workouts and ranks, react and comment, and share check-in photos with your friends only.

MORE
- GPS walks, runs and rides with a Lock Screen Live Activity
- Personal records, weekly stats and a consistency calendar
- Home and Lock Screen widget, with steps from Apple Health
- 75+ workouts across strength, cardio, HIIT, yoga, mobility and sports

IRON PILLAR PRO
- Daily readiness score from HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and training load
- Full Apple Health & Apple Watch sync: HRV, sleep, resting heart rate, and workouts saved to Health
- Advanced strength analytics: 1RM trends, next-session targets and plateau alerts
- Calorie tracking with a daily goal
- Import workouts, weigh-ins and meals with AI
- AI-written details for your custom workouts
- Unlimited custom workouts and CSV export
- Calories in your widget
- A gold frame around your rank emblem

Pro is available as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription; the annual plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple ID and renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings.
