Outdoor tracking

Walk, run, or ride. It's all on the map.

Start a walk, run, or bike ride and Iron Pillar draws your route as you go, with a big timer, distance, and average pace you can read mid-stride.

  • Keeps recording with your screen lockedPut your phone in your pocket. Tracking continues until you tap Stop.
  • Pause for red lightsPausing really stops recording, so a water break doesn't count against your pace.
  • Counts toward your streakOutdoor sessions earn XP by distance and land in your history next to everything else.

Streaks, XP & badges

Consistency, scored

Every workout earns XP. Train 30 minutes a day, across any mix of workouts, to build a streak that multiplies your bonus. Early levels come fast, and later ones take more work.

  • BadgesUnlock them as you hit milestones, starting with The Journey Begins.
  • FriendsAdd friends by username and see their workouts, streaks, and badges in your activity feed.

Iron Pillar Pro

Know when to push. Know what to lift.

Pro turns your logs and your Apple Watch into coaching: a daily readiness score, the exact weight to aim for next session, and warnings before a lift stalls.

  • 2× XP on every workoutLevel up twice as fast while you're subscribed, streak bonuses included.
  • Daily readiness scoreA 0–100 score from your HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, and training load, with a clear call on whether to push or back off.
  • Apple Health & Apple WatchWorkouts save to Apple Health and count toward your Activity rings. Apple Watch HRV, sleep, and workouts feed your readiness score.
  • Next-session targetsDouble-progression targets for every lift: add weight when you've earned it, add a rep when you haven't.
  • 1RM trends & plateau alertsEstimated one-rep max over time for each exercise, with a flag when a lift hasn't improved in three sessions.
  • Training load & habits12 weeks of training minutes, your strength-to-conditioning mix, outdoor miles, and when you train most.
  • AI workout detailsBuild a custom workout and Pro writes the overview, estimates time and calories, lists the equipment, and adds form tips.
  • Unlimited custom workouts & data exportSave as many custom workouts as you want (free accounts get 5), and export every set you've logged to CSV.
