Health & safety

Iron Pillar is not medical advice. It's a tool for planning and tracking exercise. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or haven't exercised in a while.

  • Exercise carries a risk of injury. Use proper form, work within your limits, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.
